## A. Citizens and patients

**Time with the doctor and money**
- Average primary-care consultation is **48 seconds**, the shortest of 67 countries. This is a pooled average from studies, not a national survey — 2017 — [BMJ Open blog](https://blogs.bmj.com/bmjopen/2017/11/08/primary-care-consultations-last-less-than-5-minutes-for-half-the-worlds-population/)
- OOP is **79.31%** of current health spending — 2023 — [World Bank](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/BGD/indicator/SH.XPD.OOPC.CH.ZS?format=json&per_page=10)
- About **US$5 bn a year** is spent on treatment abroad, and about 482,000 patients went to India in 2024. This is an industry estimate. Reasons given: distrust, doubts about diagnosis, hidden charges, and fear of fake drugs — Dec 2025 — [UNB](https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/usd-5-billion-spent-abroad-annually-due-to-healthcare-gaps-in-bangladesh-dcci/175121)
- Pharmacy retail prices rose **7–140%** in a few months while mill-gate prices stayed stable. DGDA fixes prices for only about 160 essential drugs — 2024 — [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/yawning-gap-between-mill-gate-and-retail-medicine-prices)

**Too few doctors, especially in villages**
- **1.1 doctors per 10,000 people in rural areas vs 18.2 in urban areas** — 2022 notice — [CPTU](https://cptu.gov.bd/advertisement-services/details-7272.html)
- A MoHFW–WHO study found 49 providers per 10,000 people, of whom **16 were unqualified** — study 2019–20, published 2021 — [WHO Bangladesh](https://www.who.int/bangladesh/news/detail/06-04-2021-from-unqualified-to-professional-medicine-bangladesh-a-promising-journey)

**Records get lost, and patients bypass the referral system**
- Urban-poor patients' paper records get damaged or lost (crowding, floods), so they have to **repeat tests** (icddr,b, n=25) — 2016 — [ICUH abstract](https://programme.exordo.com/icuh2016/delegates/presentation/88/)
- **51.4%** of 105 surveyed doctors use paper records, and 20% use digital — 2021 — [Banglajol](https://www.banglajol.info/index.php/CMOSHMCJ/article/view/87838/57377)
- At two tertiary hospitals:
  - **59%** of patients self-referred, and about 58% did not know a referral system exists
  - bed occupancy was **148.2% (secondary)** and **137% (tertiary)**
  - 2024 — [BMC Health Serv Res](https://link.springer.com/10.1186/s12913-024-11115-8)
- Referral pilots (2014, 2016, 2019) faded. A working referral system could filter about 65% of patients at upazila and district level (2023 estimate) — 2024 — [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/opinion/readers-voice/news/we-need-referral-system-works-3554706)

**Medicines: unreadable prescriptions, fakes and antibiotics**
- A High Court order required legible or printed prescriptions — 2017 — [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/node/1342969)
- A pharmacist estimated that about 80% of prescriptions are sloppy. This is anecdotal — 2019 — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/nation/179662/illegible-prescriptions-continue-despite-high)
- About **Tk 600 crore** of counterfeit medicine circulates in a Tk 18,000 crore market. This is an industry estimate, year not shown — [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/frontpage/know-your-medicine-1243567)
- **~45% of antibiotics** were sold without a prescription in 246 Dhaka pharmacies, and only **7.56%** of those pharmacies had a registered pharmacist — published 2025 — [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12744235/)
- **50.9%** (327/642) of antibiotics were bought without a prescription (icddr,b) — 2022 — [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/b2563a5093494395a07298723c0c4801)

**Mothers and children**
- Maternal mortality is **136 per 100,000** live births. The 2020 target of 105 was missed — SVRS 2023 — [UNFPA 2024](https://asiapacific.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/pub-pdf/unfpa_fistula_bangladesh_profile_2024.pdf)
- **45% of births are C-sections**, and 84% of those are in private hospitals — BDHS 2022 — [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/615022)

**Long-term conditions, cancer screening and mental health**
- **15.8% of adults have undiagnosed hypertension**, vs 12.2% diagnosed, and **6.0% have undiagnosed diabetes** — BDHS 2017–18 — [DOAJ/BMC 2023](https://doaj.org/article/8e696c7abbe4486187d630b469aa8f43); [Frontiers 2022](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2022.1066449/epub)
- Cervical (VIA) screening coverage is about **11.3%** — 2017–22 — [BMC Public Health 2023](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12889-023-17545-z)
- Mental illness affects **16.8% of adults**, and **92.3% of them get no treatment** — survey 2018–19 — [WHO Bangladesh](https://www.who.int/bangladesh/news/detail/27-11-2019-minister-of-health-releases-first-findings-of-national-mental-health-survey)

**Emergencies, ambulances and blood**
- The 999 hotline is free but the ambulance is not — 2017 — [Dhaka Tribune](https://archive.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/2017/12/07/999-emergency-hotline-launch)
- Dhaka has **no centralised emergency medical service (EMS)** — [University of Toronto](https://che.utoronto.ca/?p=1102)
- An ambulance syndicate operates at public hospitals: for example, **Tk 6,000 from DMCH vs Tk 3,000** for an outside ambulance — [Daily Star](https://tds-images.thedailystar.net/ds/unheard-voices/news/how-rescue-bangladesh-ambulance-mafia-3973986)
- Blood demand is about **1.0–1.1 million bags a year**, and only about 30% comes from voluntary donors — DGHS, c.2020 — [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/backpage/news/blood-banks-may-run-dry-1890727)
- There is "no single, verified national platform" for blood — 2025 — [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/thoughts/blood-donation-goes-digital-lessons-global-best-practices-1164731)

**Unsafe or unlicensed facilities**
- More than **880** unauthorised hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centres and blood banks were closed — 2024 — [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/views/editorial/news/dghs-raids-illegal-clinics-expose-the-rot-health-sector-3035386)
- About 1,027 facilities were without valid licences (DGHS report) — 2024 — [Daily Star](https://online91.thedailystar.net/opinion/editorial/news/rein-errant-clinics-diagnostic-centres-4135366)

**Free medicines run out at community clinics**
- About **84%** of health workers say patients come back because medicines are unavailable — Jun 2026 — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/health/413839/inside-bangladesh%E2%80%99s-community-clinics-promise)
- Jashore: **283 clinics** had no medicine for about 8 months — [Bonik Barta](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/n79GkolkDClJDR5X)

**Can people use digital tools? (This drives design)**
- Literacy is **77.7%**, 74.6% rural — Dec 2024 — [Jagonews24](https://www.jagonews24.com/en/sci-tech/news/78924)
- Smartphone use is **41% urban and 26% rural** (individual use). Illiteracy is the reason given by 28% of urban and 19% of rural non-users — GSMA 2024 — [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1013061)
- **73.4%** of households own a smartphone and 57.4% have internet. Phones are often shared, so design for caregiver/proxy use — BBS, Jan–Mar 2026 — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/415972/bbs-survey-over-57%25-of-bangladeshi-households-now)

**Climate**
- Dengue killed **1,705 people with 321,179 cases**, the worst year on record — 2023 — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/bangladesh/al1f09qaaf)
- The heatwave caused 15 confirmed heatstroke deaths in 14 days. This is an undercount — 2024 — [UNB](https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/heatstroke-claims-15-lives-in-14-days-dghs/135079)

**People with disabilities**
- Accessibility measures were inadequate across 150 public facilities in 16 districts — 2019 study, published 2022 — [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9490997/)

**Privacy law**
- The **Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025** was gazetted on 6 Nov 2025:
  - health data counts as "sensitive personal data"
  - explicit consent is needed before collecting, storing, transferring or using it
  - some sections start 18 months after gazetting, about May 2027 (researchers' estimate)
  - [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1281356); [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/tech-startup/news/bangladeshs-personal-data-protection-ordinance-2025-key-takeaways-4015401)
- Enforcement status under the 2026 elected government is **[uncertain]**.

**Gaps (no data found):** national misdiagnosis rate; waiting times in public outpatient departments (OPD); recent counterfeit-prevalence study; official 999 ambulance tariff.

## B. Doctors, nurses, community health workers (CHWs), pharmacists, lab staff

**Workload and vacancies**
- Rural physicians see a mean of **~30 patients a day** — 2017 (via search abstract) — [BMC HSR](https://bmchealthservres.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12913-017-2722-1/tables/1)
- **450–500 outpatients a day** at Keraniganj UHC. **55.2%** of upazila medical officer posts were vacant (4,770 doctors for 10,640 posts) — 2018 — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/Unhealthy-state-of-health-services-for-rural)
- Bauphal UHC has 4 of 15 doctor posts filled for about 4.5 lakh people — Oct 2025 (via search abstract) — [Observer](https://observerbd.com/news/547528)
- Interns at Bogura's 500-bed hospital say they serve about **3,000 patients** — Jun 2026 — [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1465051)
- Doctors, nurses and midwives together number **12.78 per 10,000** vs WHO's 44.5. The doctor:nurse:midwife ratio is 1:0.75:0.74 vs WHO's 1:3:5. **About 72,000 posts are vacant**, and 46% of nurses want to work abroad — Jun 2026 — [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/opinion/editorial/news/shortage-nurses-urgent-crisis-4203666)
- Absenteeism is **35%** on average — 2003/2004 data — [World Bank](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/bb381354-fd28-5ee4-940f-6171ac390013/full)

**Burnout and violence**
- **55.4%** of frontline doctors in Chattogram are burnt out — 2020 data, published 2022 — [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9681110/)
- In a CIPRB/ICRC survey of 1,703 health staff:
  - **more than 80%** had experienced or witnessed violence
  - 71.9% of it was by attendants or relatives
  - **only ~25% reported it**
  - 2023 survey, cited Jun 2026 — [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1465051)
- **67.3%** of 441 DMCH physicians have faced workplace violence — BMJ Open 2024, cited in [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1465051)
- Of 157 incidents posted on the "Platform" doctors' network, 61% happened at night, and interns and emergency doctors were hit most — 2021 — [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.787221/full)
- A government emergency alarm ("Pagla Ghonta" / "Code Blue") pilot was announced for DMCH and Shaheed Suhrawardy MCH. It is not confirmed as live — May 2026 — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/health/410642/health-minister-emergency-safety-alarms-to-be)

**No decision support, no continuing education (CME), double reporting**
- Treatment guidelines are published only as PDFs. The NCD desk guide for upazila doctors dates from 2015 — [DGHS](https://old.dghs.gov.bd/index.php/en/publications/guideline); [WHO NCD desk guide](https://extranet.who.int/ncdccs/Data/BGD_D1_BGD_NCD%20Desk%20Guide_2015.pdf)
- The national formulary was not updated for 8 years after 2006 **[uncertain on date]** — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/feature/travel/61695/no-national-formulary-in-eight-years)
- BMDC licence renewal is paperwork only, with **no CME credits** — [BMDC](https://www.bmdc.org.bd/reg-renewal)
- BMDC figures:
  - 134,568 registered physicians
  - **~36,000 practising without renewed registration**
  - only 147 complaints since 2010
  - Nov 2024 — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/jkx47oy065)
- Staff keep paper and electronic DHIS2 systems side by side. Parallel reporting "increases the reporting workload" — 2020 — [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7249629/)
- Why patients are referred to tertiary hospitals: inadequate facilities 53%, inadequate treatment 47%, no expert physician 31% — 2024 (via search abstract) — [BMC HSR PDF](https://bmchealthservres.biomedcentral.com/counter/pdf/10.1186/s12913-024-11115-8.pdf)
- At Savar UHC, dengue referrals were held back by an ambulance shortage and **no feedback system**. 25% of 36 referred patients were unhappy with communication — [BRACU](https://dspace.bracu.ac.bd/items/3cfefc17-e696-48d8-80c1-b06aa715a7c0)

**Community clinics, village doctors and drug shops**
- About **70 patients a day per community clinic** (researchers' arithmetic from 2,238 patients across 32 clinics) — 2021 — [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/ecd5cdd2b63a49848b10cfe31a41f8ee)
- About 490,000 people use community clinics daily. Most clinics have no MBBS doctor, so CHCPs (community health care providers) deliver the care — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/health/413839/inside-bangladesh%E2%80%99s-community-clinics-promise)
- 14,213 of 15,564 community clinics use a DHIS2 tracker for cervical screening registration — Apr 2024 — [BMC Public Health 2025](https://researchexchange.ocrahope.org/papers/s12889-025-22668-6)
- More than **65%** of people get first-line care from village doctors — 2018 — [JOGH](https://jogha.org/documents/issue201802/jogh-08-020413.htm)
- For Rangpur inpatients, the first contact was an informal provider for 46.2% — 2026 — [Banglajol](https://banglajol.info/index.php/JBCPS/article/view/91557)
- About **50%** of drug sellers lack formal pharmacy training — [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12142531/)

**Lab and diagnostic staff**
- 32,526 of 92,294 medical-technologist posts are vacant — undated — [Bonik Barta](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/jlhin00R6bKyinX4)
- No medical technologists were recruited in government hospitals in the 11 years to 2020 — [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/city/news/wholl-run-the-tests-1894342)
- Technologists in post: **3,892 vs 6,406 sanctioned** — 2026 — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/904ggpi81h)

## C. Medical students, interns, residents

**Seats and internship**
- **11,101 MBBS seats** in 2025–26 (5,100 government, 6,001 private). **572 seats were cut** for poor infrastructure and staffing — Jan 2026 — [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/step-towards-improving-medical-education-quality-4082026)
- Internship assessment is mostly a logbook signature: **>66% of interns** and 72% of doctors say so, and ~54% of interns report no assessment after major rotations (8 colleges) — [BJME](https://banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/32243)
- About 56% of interns are negative about how the logbook is used in each rotation (9 hospitals) — [BJME](https://www.banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/36233)
- Counterpoint: 400 interns gave mean agreement above 4 out of 5 — 2021–22 — [BJME](https://www.banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/63680)
- Habiganj MC ran for about 7 years with no practical training for interns — 2025 — [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/opinion/news/ensure-quality-medical-education-4034436)

**Postgraduate bottleneck and pay**
- About **3,500 postgraduate seats vs 11,000+ graduates** a year (about 1:10). Fresh graduates start on BDT 15–20k a month — 2024 — [Daily Star Campus](https://www.thedailystar.net/campus/news/do-we-have-too-many-medical-colleges-3724766)
- FCPS Part I pass rates were **14.6–18.4%** in 2007–2011. No recent figures were found — [JBCPS](https://banglajol.info/index.php/JBCPS/article/view/415/422)
- About 7,000 postgraduate trainees and 3,500 interns went on strike over allowances (Tk 20k and Tk 15k) — Mar 2024 — [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/health/postgraduate-trainees-interns-go-strike-over-pay-hike-815036)
- The allowance was reportedly raised to Tk 35k from 1 Jul 2025 **[uncertain]** — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/369471/allowance-for-trainee-doctors-to-increase)

**Wellbeing and safety**
- **28.8% have depression** (840 students), 65% anxiety — 2021 survey — [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10904939/)
- A preprint reports 38.9% depression and 17.6% suicidal ideation. It is not peer reviewed — 2025 — [medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.01.30.25321378.full.pdf)
- **32.9%** are burnt out — [NEMJ](https://journal.nemc.edu.bd/?p=6878)
- A High Court order required anti-ragging committees, but most institutions did not comply — 2020 order — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/opinion/editorial/ox6e5sxbvn)

**Cost and foreign students**
- A private MBBS costs **BDT 30–45 lakh in total** (see corrections) — 2024 — [Daily Star Campus](https://www.thedailystar.net/campus/news/do-we-have-too-many-medical-colleges-3724766)
- Foreign students in private colleges fell from a peak of 2,096 to **1,187** in 2024–25 — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/kh2l2zl57m)

**Online learning, nursing and MATS**
- During COVID online classes, students cited poor internet (67%), data cost (65%) and ineffective practical teaching (59%) — 2021 — [SEAJME](https://seajme.sljol.info/articles/10.4038/seajme.v18i2.637)
- Nursing institutions grew from 87 (2010) to **453 (2025)**, 383 of them private — [Bonik Barta](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/2AkGPWv89oGNdGYp)
- Only 2 of 10 private nursing colleges in Mirpur have their own hospital — [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/opinion/editorial/news/nursing-colleges-are-struggling-amid-poor-oversight-3447461)
- MATS (medical assistant training schools): 16 government and 200+ private, with weak quality oversight — 2025 — [Daily Star PDF](https://epfs.thedailystar.net/TDS/2025/03/20/Bangladesh/CAM/5_07/0353045d247e4846_07.pdf)

## D. Medical faculty

**Vacancies and training**
- **43%** of teaching posts in government colleges are vacant — 2025 — [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/opinion/news/ensure-quality-medical-education-4034436)
- DGME data: 2,459 of 5,920 posts vacant (≈41.5%), and ≈30% vacant in basic subjects — 2024 — [Daily Star Campus](https://www.thedailystar.net/campus/news/do-we-have-too-many-medical-colleges-3724766)
- **62%** of teachers had no faculty-development activity in the previous year — [BJME](https://www.banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/49245)
- 95% of 331 teachers had teaching-methodology training, mostly through CME Dhaka (Centre for Medical Education) — [BJME](https://www.banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/87079/56477)

**Exams are inconsistent**
- Mean practical scores: OSPE 77.7 vs traditional 64.4, with **significant differences between colleges** — the same exam is not marked the same way everywhere — [IMSEAR](https://imsear.searo.who.int/handle/123456789/217448)
- An evaluation of BCPS exams found traditional assessment is "largely subjective", with examiners grading the same performance differently — [BJME](https://banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/32190)

**Accreditation**
- BMEAC, the new accreditation body, has accredited only **2 colleges** (DMC and Shaheed Suhrawardy MC). About 100 colleges remain, and BMEAC's own WFME recognition is pending — Jan 2026 — [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/step-towards-improving-medical-education-quality-4082026)
- Without WFME recognition, degrees may not be recognised abroad — [JDMC](https://banglajol.info/index.php/JDMC/article/view/83429)

**Research and teaching resources**
- At least **325 papers** by Bangladeshi researchers were retracted from 2019 to June 2026 — [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/education/turning-research-into-a-farce)
- Medicine has no anti-plagiarism policy, and promotion is tied to publication counts — [J Medicine](https://banglajol.info/index.php/JOM/article/view/8422/6254)
- Body donations at BSMMU are 4–5 a year, and some years zero (via search abstract) — [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/features/panorama/between-life-and-death-do-you-think-about-organ-donation-789166)
- During COVID, 85% of teachers named practical and bedside teaching as the main barrier — 2021 — [SEAJME](https://seajme.sljol.info/articles/10.4038/seajme.v18i2.637)

## E. Institutions and administrators

**The e-Health Card is not ready on the ground**
- Civil surgeons in the pilot districts had **no instructions**, and facilities lack computers, internet, servers and trained staff — Apr 2026 — [Bonik Barta](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/SyidD595PYwTVnF0)

**Broken or idle equipment** (PPRC study of 22 facilities in 8 divisions, 2026)
- **52%** of diagnostic equipment was unused in the past year
- **79%** of service gaps were caused by malfunctioning equipment
- imaging was available 65% of the time but provided regularly only 25%
