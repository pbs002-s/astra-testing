# 04 — Medical education and institutions: what other countries have that Bangladesh lacks, and what can be modernised

Plain-language summary. Bangladesh's medical colleges and hospitals mostly do not lack buildings or hardware. Eight public colleges got simulation labs and digital dissection tables in 2023, about 100 public hospitals run OpenMRS+, and DHIS2 covers 14,000+ community clinics. What they lack is the **workflow software around them**: who signs off a procedure, how an exam is scored fairly, which machine is broken, where a referred patient went, and who is waiting for which machine.

Labels used below:
- **[uncertain]** — the researchers could not fully verify the claim.
- **(via search abstract)** — the figure came from a search-engine summary, not the full page.

### 2.2 What Bangladesh already has

Do not rebuild these. Plug into them instead.

- **DHIS2** (the national health-reporting system) — the world's largest deployment, about 98% reporting, 14,000+ community clinics — [UNICEF](https://www.unicef.org/rosa/media/3416/file/A%20Case%20Study%20Bangladesh%20Online.pdf); [DHIS2.org](https://dhis2.org/expanding-routine-data-use-bangladesh/)
- **OpenMRS+ with Bahmni and OpenELIS** (hospital records and lab software) in about 100 public hospitals. This figure comes from an informal DGHS source — [DGHS git](https://git.dghs.gov.bd/mahfuj/chatbot-knowledge/src/commit/b25b6fd7d637782fdfa7f503c6d031baf6dd734f/openmrs.md)
- **Health ID** — one per person, verified against the NID or birth registration number, and it starts with "9". The Shared Health Record (SHR) runs on OpenHIE — [SHR UHID](https://en.info.shr.dghs.gov.bd/uhidmci/)
- **BD Core FHIR R4 IG** with a public sandbox at `https://sandbox.fhir.dghs.gov.bd/fhir` — [fhir.dghs.gov.bd](https://fhir.dghs.gov.bd/)
- **DGHS eLMIS** for medicine logistics — [elmis.dghs.gov.bd](https://elmis.dghs.gov.bd/)
- **IMCI app** for child illness, at all 319 Barishal facilities — [Save the Children](https://www.savethechildren.net/bangladesh/stories/over-300-govt-healthcare-facilities-barishal-will-digitize-treatment-plans)
- **Muktopaath** e-learning, which hosted the DGHS COVID course for doctors — [teachers.gov.bd](https://teachers.gov.bd/content/details/543477)
- **Shasthya Batayon 16263** health hotline — 27M+ calls since it started, but it is in a funding crisis — [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/health/natl-telehealth-helpline-16263-faces-shutdown-amid-funding-crisis)

## Part 3 — What can realistically be modernised, and how hard it is

| Area | Today | Modernisation | Foreign precedent | Difficulty | Hackathon-ready? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Internship assessment | Paper logbook, signature only | Offline-first e-logbook. The supervisor signs off with a one-time code (OTP) or QR, entries map to rotation competencies, and the internship committee gets a dashboard | Horus (UK) | Low (tech), medium (BMDC adoption) | **Yes** (Idea 8 in `05_final_ideas.md`) |
| Practical exams | Paper checklists, variation between colleges | Tablet scoring that flags examiner variance and sets pass marks by borderline regression | Qpercom, COES | Low | **Yes** (Idea 10) |
| Emergency-skills refresher | Face-to-face HBB only | Bangla scenario game for neonatal resuscitation | LIFE, HBB Prompt | Medium (content needs clinical review) | Reserve idea |
| Exam prep | Q-banks and coaching | Spaced repetition mapped to the BCPS exam blueprint | Marrow | Low | Weak e-Health Card fit |
| Accreditation | Paper self-assessment against 11 criteria | Evidence tracker for BMEAC's criteria | — | Low | Weak e-Health Card fit |
| Outpatient registration | Paper or desk entry | QR Health-ID scan-and-share | ABDM | Low | **Yes** (Idea 1) |
| Referral | No feedback loop | Closed-loop e-referral: specialist advice, then back-referral to the original facility | Vula, Telessaúde, MOPH Refer | Medium | **Yes** (Idea 2) |
| Lab results | Paper report collected in person | OpenELIS sends the result to the Health ID and to the patient by SMS | Zambia SMS | Low | **Yes** (Idea 3) |
| Equipment | Repairs by letter and tender | QR asset register, breakdown tickets, uptime dashboard | MEMMS | Low | **Yes** (Idea 6) |
| Radiotherapy and diagnostics queues | Separate paper lists at each centre | One shared national waiting list with capacity view | SISREG (as a lesson) | Medium | **Yes** (Idea 5) |
| Blood | Donor matching only | Stock visibility across transfusion centres | e-RaktKosh | Medium | Reserve idea |
