## 7. The strongest signal for what judges want

> [SITE] "...this three-day programme at BICC unites innovators, universities, global health communities and industry to spark, test and scale **solutions that power the national e-Health Card**." — [About](https://ehealthhackathonbd.org/about)

This is the clearest statement of what the organisers want. A pitch that shows how your MVP makes the **e-Health Card / Health ID useful at a real service point** (registration, referral, lab result, pharmacy, ambulance) matches the event's stated purpose.

The homepage quotes from leadership point the same way, and are worth echoing in a pitch — all from [Home](https://ehealthhackathonbd.org/):
- **Prime Minister:** "prevention is better than cure … healthcare begins in the home and community"
- **On corruption:** "every penny allocated to the health sector will be spent transparently". Tools that make money, stock or equipment visible fit this message.
- **On primary care:** a "Primary Healthcare Unit in every union and urban ward" and "100,000 additional community health workers"
