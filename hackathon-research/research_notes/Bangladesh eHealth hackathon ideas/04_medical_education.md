# Medical Education in Bangladesh (students, interns, residents, faculty) vs. international practice, plus foreign med-ed technologies and their Bangladesh status

Scope: Phase-2 groups C (students/interns/residents: MBBS, BDS, nursing, MATS/paramedic, pharmacy, PG trainees) and D (faculty), plus Phase-3(b) innovations. Research date: Oct 2026. "Search summary" means the figure came from a search-engine extract of the cited page, which I did not open in full. Treat those as slightly weaker than "fetched".

## A1. How many colleges, seats and teachers are there, and what is the accreditation status?

### Takeaway
Bangladesh has about 103–104 medical colleges (37 public, 66–67 private) offering about 11,100 MBBS seats (2025–26). Around 41–43% of public-college teaching posts are vacant. In 2025–26 the ministry cut 572 seats and suspended enrolment at 6 private colleges over infrastructure and teacher shortages. National accreditation moved to a new body, BMEAC (2023 Act). In Jan 2026 BMEAC accredited the first two colleges (DMC, Shaheed Suhrawardy MC), but BMEAC's own WFME recognition was still pending.

### Cited Findings
- **2025–26 admission circular:** 5,100 seats in 37 government colleges and 6,001 seats in 66 private colleges, 11,101 in total. 572 seats were cut for 2025–26 over "inadequate infrastructure, manpower, hospital facilities". — [Daily Star, 16 Jan 2026 (fetched)](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/step-towards-improving-medical-education-quality-4082026)
- **Change from the previous year:** government seats fell from 5,380 to 5,100 and private seats from 6,293 to 6,001. Enrolment was suspended at six private colleges. A new 50-seat private college was still approved. The previous government had added 1,030 seats for 2023–24. — [Daily Star editorial, 14 Nov 2025 (fetched)](https://www.thedailystar.net/opinion/news/ensure-quality-medical-education-4034436)
- **Public-college vacancies (2025):** "43 percent of teaching posts in government medical colleges are vacant", citing a March 2025 report ("Teacher crisis plagues public medical colleges"). Habiganj Medical College has run about 7 years with no practical-training opportunities for interns. Students at Sher-e-Bangla MC, Barishal protested an acute teacher shortage in 2025. — [Daily Star, 14 Nov 2025](https://www.thedailystar.net/opinion/news/ensure-quality-medical-education-4034436)
- **DGME data (2024):** 2,459 of 5,920 required teaching posts were vacant (≈41.5%). In basic subjects, 588 of 2,005 posts were vacant (≈30%). One public Dhaka college's Forensic Medicine department had a single Assistant Professor. — [Daily Star Campus, 10 Oct 2024 (fetched)](https://www.thedailystar.net/campus/news/do-we-have-too-many-medical-colleges-3724766)
- **Private-seat vacancies (2024):** about 20% of private seats were vacant. The Bangladesh Private Medical College Association says 1,200; the ministry says about 635, up from 242 in 2022–23. Over 11,000 graduates a year. Starting salary for a fresh graduate is BDT 15,000–20,000 a month. — [Daily Star Campus, 10 Oct 2024](https://www.thedailystar.net/campus/news/do-we-have-too-many-medical-colleges-3724766)
- **2023–24 cycle:** 104,374 applicants competed for 11,675 seats at 104 colleges. — search summary of [Daily Star/TBS reports via search](https://www.thedailystar.net/campus/news/do-we-have-too-many-medical-colleges-3724766) [uncertain on exact source page]
- **May 2026:** the BNP government approved its first new public medical college, with seven more planned, "as many established medical schools grapple with severe shortages of teachers and infrastructure". — search summary of Daily Star coverage via [Daily Star tag page](https://www.thedailystar.net/tags/medical-education-bangladesh) [uncertain: article not opened]
- **Accreditation:** BMEAC was set up in April 2024 under the Bangladesh Medical Education Accreditation Act 2023 and reconstituted in Oct 2024. It uses 11 criteria aligned with WFME standards. In Jan 2026 it accredited the MBBS programmes of DMC and Shaheed Suhrawardy MC for 5 years. Six more colleges had submitted self-assessments. A WFME team visited DMC. BMEAC's own WFME accreditation was "pending" with a "positive response". — [Daily Star, 16 Jan 2026](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/step-towards-improving-medical-education-quality-4082026)
- **Recognition risk:** without WFME accreditation, Bangladeshi MBBS degrees may not be recognised in several countries (the ECFMG/WFME 2024 rule). — [JDMC editorial, BanglaJOL](https://banglajol.info/index.php/JDMC/article/view/83429)

### Inferences
- Accreditation is new and runs on paper self-assessment reports across 11 criteria. About 100 colleges still need it, so evidence collection and gap tracking for accreditation is a real unmet tool need for faculty.
- With teaching posts 41–43% vacant, faculty time is the scarce resource. Tools that save faculty hours (auto-marking, question banks, digital logbook sign-offs) address a measured problem.

### Gaps
- I found no official student:teacher ratio per college. BMDC's prescribed ratio was not found.
- I found no list of private colleges lacking an attached hospital that meets BMDC bed requirements.

## A2. What are the anatomy, cadaver and simulation conditions?

### Takeaway
Cadavers come mainly from unclaimed forensic bodies. Voluntary donation is tiny: BSMMU/BMU gets about 4–5 donations a year, and zero in the pandemic years. A government rollout put simulation labs with a "digital dissection table" and e-libraries in 8 colleges (April 2023) and promised staged expansion. A few private colleges and one WHO-backed lab in Cox's Bazar have skills labs. Access is patchy.

### Cited Findings
- Most cadavers come from forensic mortuaries as unclaimed bodies, plus some donations. The Anatomy Act provides for supplying unclaimed bodies. — [Bangladesh J Anatomy review (2010)](https://www.banglajol.info/index.php/BJA/article/view/7023)
- **Donations:** a BSMMU official reports 4–5 body donations a year, with some years (all three pandemic years) at zero, which is "not adequate". Barriers are socio-cultural and religious beliefs and lack of awareness. — search summary of [TBS feature on organ/body donation](https://www.tbsnews.net/features/panorama/between-life-and-death-do-you-think-about-organ-donation-789166) (early 2020s)
- **Bone supply:** skeleton and bone shortages followed India's 1985 export ban, while student numbers rose sharply. — [Daily Star "Skeleton trouble"](https://www.thedailystar.net/frontpage/skeleton-trouble-1356085) (older)
- **Government rollout:** on 15 Apr 2023 simulation labs and e-libraries were inaugurated in 8 medical colleges, including Manikganj Col. Maleque MC. The labs use an "advanced digital dissection table" and are to be extended "in stages" to all medical and dental colleges. The article does not name the vendor or the other 7 colleges. — [Dhaka Tribune, 15 Apr 2023 (fetched)](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/309069/simulation-labs-e-libraries-at-8-medical-colleges)
- **Cox's Bazar:** a WHO and Government of Bangladesh multidisciplinary skills lab opened at Cox's Bazar Medical College in 2022, starting as an obstetric/PPH skills lab. — [WHO Bangladesh, 19 Jul 2022](https://www.who.int/bangladesh/news/detail/19-07-2022-new-initiative-the-cox-s-bazar-multidisciplinary-skills-lab)
- **Private colleges:** Holy Family Red Crescent MC runs a dry lab with manikins (intubation, LP, IV/IM) and rotations from 3rd year. — [HFRCMC](https://hfrcmc.edu.bd/content.php?f=Dry-Lab). Khulna/KYAMC runs a Medical Skill Centre (IV cannulation, catheterisation, BLS, airway). — [KYAMC](https://kyamc.edu.bd/facilities/15)

### Inferences
- A "digital dissection table" exists in at least 8 public colleges (vendor unverified, so possibly Anatomage or a Chinese clone [uncertain]). Hardware is not the gap. Content in the local curriculum, scheduled access and assessment around it are the gaps.
- Low donation rates point to a body-donation registry and awareness MVP (pledge, consent, next-of-kin workflow). This is non-generic, but its policy dependency is high.

### Gaps
- No national count of colleges with functioning skills labs. No cadaver-per-student ratio.
- The vendor of the 2023 "digital dissection table" is unverified.

## A3. What do the curriculum, assessment, logbooks and internship look like?

### Takeaway
MBBS Curriculum 2021 (rolled out Aug 2022) revised the 2012 curriculum, with competency-based subject operational manuals, integrated teaching and WHO support. Assessment still relies on written, oral viva and structured practical exams (OSPE/OSCE in some). Internship (1 year) uses a paper logbook. Studies show weak post-rotation assessment: "logbook signature" is the main assessment method for more than two-thirds of interns.

### Cited Findings
- **Origin:** Curriculum 2021 is a revision of Curriculum 2012, done by CME, DGHS, BM&DC, MOH&FW and the deans with WHO Bangladesh support, after the first 2012 batch graduated in 2019. Its stated aim is "need-based, community-oriented, competent" graduates. — [BMDC Curriculum 2021 front page](https://www.bmdc.org.bd/docs/curriculum/2021/1.FrontPage.pdf); [DGME operational manual (2023)](https://dgme.portal.gov.bd/sites/default/files/files/dgme.portal.gov.bd/page/e2f6cb3c_fadc_4510_95fa_9d4dae4990cd/2023-10-10-13-22-c5693b76bb778b2958954b2807b4b74d.pdf)
- **Structure:** the subject operational manuals list competencies, integrated teaching, formative and summative assessment, in-course evaluation and the professional exam format. Implementation started in Aug 2022. There are 4 phases over a 5-year course. — [DGME manuals](https://dgme.portal.gov.bd/sites/default/files/files/dgme.portal.gov.bd/page/e2f6cb3c_fadc_4510_95fa_9d4dae4990cd/2023-10-10-13-36-79ac3a469c30b05d95d99d7452262e55.pdf); [JMC Biochemistry OM](https://jmc.gov.bd/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Biochemistry-OM.pdf)
- **OSPE vs traditional (physiology):** a study of 400 students in 4 colleges found a mean OSPE score of ≈77.7 vs ≈64.4 for traditional practicals, with significant differences in OSPE scores between colleges (a standardisation problem). — [IMSEAR/WHO SEARO index](https://imsear.searo.who.int/handle/123456789/217448)
- **BCPS OSPE evaluation:** an evaluation of OSPE in FCPS Part II (Obs/Gyn) states that traditional assessment is "largely subjective" and that different examiners grade the same performance differently. — [BJME, BanglaJOL](https://banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/32190)
- **Internship assessment:** a study of 8 colleges (250 interns, 50 supervisors) found no assessment after major rotations (≈54% of interns, 24% of teachers said so). The logbook signature was the main assessment method (>66% of interns, 72% of doctors). Most said 1 year is too short and suggested about 18 months. — [BJME (Noman et al.)](https://banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/32243); [BJME duration study](https://banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/52307)
- **Performance appraisal:** in 9 hospitals (445 interns, 52 teachers), ≈54% were negative about the performance-appraisal system and ≈56% were negative about logbook use in each rotation. The authors recommended proper logbook use, feedback and a monitoring committee. — [BJME (Rahim et al.)](https://www.banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/36233)
- **Counterpoint (2021–22):** a survey of 400 interns gave the logbook-based programme mean agreement above 4 out of 5. — [BJME](https://www.banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/63680)
- **Rotatory logbook:** BMDC piloted the rotatory internship logbook at Sylhet MAG Osmani MCH for 2 years, then extended it to all colleges. — [BMDC Rotatory Internship Programme PDF](https://www.bmdc.org.bd/docs/RotatoryInternshipProgramme.pdf)

### Inferences
- An offline-first digital logbook/e-portfolio is a strong, non-generic MVP. It would let interns log a procedure, have the supervisor sign off by QR or OTP, map entries to BMDC rotation competencies, and give a dashboard to the internship committee. The data above directly supports the need (signature-only assessment, no post-rotation assessment, the monitoring committee recommendation), and India's PGMER-2023 e-logbook mandate is a regional precedent (see B).
- Between-college variance in OSPE scores supports a digital OSCE/OSPE scoring tool: tablet checklists, examiner-variance analytics and standard setting.

### Gaps
- Curriculum 2021's exact assessment weights and the extent of OSCE adoption at the professional-exam level were not verified.
- No data on whether any college uses a digital logbook. None was found.

## A4. What is the postgraduate picture (BCPS FCPS, BMU MD/MS residency, seats, pass rates, allowances)?

### Takeaway
There are about 3,500 PG seats against more than 11,000 graduates a year (≈1:10, 2024). FCPS Part I historically passes only about 14–18% of candidates (2007–2011 data; no recent public figures found). Trainee allowances drove repeated national strikes: in March 2024 about 7,000 PG trainees and 3,500 interns struck for Tk50k/Tk30k against Tk20k/Tk15k. Increments followed to Tk30k (Dec 2024) and reportedly Tk35k from 1 July 2025. BCPS publishes trainee allocation by merit, preference and post availability.

### Cited Findings
- **Seats (2024):** 2,042 PG seats in 20 national institutes and 10 medical colleges, plus about 1,300–1,400 more including FCPS, for about 3,500 in total. That is an undergraduate:PG ratio of about 10:1. PG trainee pay is about BDT 30,000 a month. — [Daily Star Campus, 10 Oct 2024](https://www.thedailystar.net/campus/news/do-we-have-too-many-medical-colleges-3724766)
- **Residency entrance:** 8,498 candidates sat the BSMMU residency test for 1,048 seats across 5 faculties. — [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/172996) [uncertain year, probably 2020–21]. BSMMU was renamed Bangladesh Medical University (BMU) in 2025 (advisory council, 13 Mar 2025; ordinance reported 13 Apr 2025; sources differ). — [bdnews24](https://bdnews24.com/bangladesh/4dd003f79e60); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Medical_University)
- **FCPS Part I pass rates:** Jan 2007 16.4% (759/4,630); Jul 2007 16.4% (812/4,949); Jan 2008 14.6% (571/3,905); Jul 2008 18.4%; Jan 2009 14.9%; Jan 2011 16.2% (512/3,161). — [J Bangladesh Coll Phys Surg results, BanglaJOL](https://banglajol.info/index.php/JBCPS/article/view/415/422) (and sibling issues). No 2019–2026 figures found.
- **Exam format:** FCPS Part I is MCQ plus SBA only, with no viva or practical. — [BCPS marking system](https://bcps.edu.bd/bcpscms/upload/FCPS-I,%20Marking%20system%20(final).pdf). Online application is mandatory for all BCPS exams. — [BCPS notice](https://bcps.edu.bd/document/ExamNotification.pdf)
- **Trainee allocation:** placement ranks by merit, institute preference, available training posts and trainer availability, and trainees upload a joining letter. — [BCPS Trainee Allocation Result July 2026](https://bcps.edu.bd/bcpscms/upload/Revised%20Notice%20for%20Trainee%20Allocation%20Result%20July%202026.pdf)
- **March 2024 strike:** about 7,000 PG trainees and 3,500 interns struck for 48 hours. Demands were Tk50,000 for PG trainees (from Tk20,000) and Tk30,000 for interns (from Tk15,000), plus a law against violence on doctors. At DMCH, 240 interns and 1,000 PG trainees took part. — [TBS, 24 Mar 2024 (fetched)](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/health/postgraduate-trainees-interns-go-strike-over-pay-hike-815036)
- **Dec 2024:** after the Shahbagh blockade on 22 Dec 2024, a 23 Dec notification raised the stipend of unpaid BCPS trainees who had passed Part I by Tk5,000 to Tk30,000. Trainees rejected it. — search summary of [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/666346) and [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/trainee-doctors-block-shahbagh-again-to-demand-hike-in-allowance)
- **July 2025:** a later notification reportedly raised the allowance to Tk35,000 effective 1 July 2025. It applies to resident and non-resident private or unpaid trainees at BSMMU-affiliated institutions. — search summary of [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/369471/allowance-for-trainee-doctors-to-increase) [uncertain: not opened]
- **Unpaid status:** private trainees are not on the government payroll, provide clinical service and are barred from other jobs. — search summary of [TBS deep dive](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/817021)
- **Allocation complaints:** Chattogram MC interns protested a ministry FCPS-training decision that restricted placements, mandated upazila service and limited allowances. — [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/ctg-medical-college-interns-demand-withdrawal-fcps-training-decision-warn-work-stoppage) (date not verified)

### Inferences
- About 11,000 candidates a year compete for about 3,500 seats in a Part I exam that is MCQ/SBA only, a large and under-served exam-prep market. A Bangla-context, BCPS-blueprint adaptive question bank (a "Marrow for FCPS") is viable as an MVP. Note, however, that informal coaching and question banks exist (see B), so differentiate with spaced repetition, analytics and past-paper mapping.
- An allocation-transparency dashboard (seat matrix, closing merit, trainer availability) mirrors India's MCC counselling. It depends on BCPS data.

### Gaps
- "Honorary medical officer" as a Bangladesh term: no source found. The term appears for Pakistan (Punjab). Bangladesh uses "private/unpaid trainee".
- No recent (2019–2026) FCPS or residency pass rates. No official applicant:seat figures for 2025–26.

## A5. What is known about student welfare (mental health, ragging), cost, and foreign students?

### Takeaway
Depression affects about 29–39% of Bangladeshi medical students and burnout about 33%. Ragging and political-wing abuse in hostels is documented, and a 2020 High Court order for anti-ragging committees is largely ignored. Private MBBS costs about BDT 20–45 lakh in total. Foreign enrolment in private colleges fell from a peak of 2,096 to 1,187 (2024–25).

### Cited Findings
- **Depression:** 28.8% in 840 students across 4 colleges (2021 survey); anxiety 65%; perceived stress 85%. Predictors were poor sleep, academic performance and relationships. — [PMC10904939 (F1000Research 2022)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10904939/)
- A preprint (MINI interview, Dhaka) reports 38.9% depression and 17.6% suicidal ideation (secondhand figure, not peer reviewed). — [medRxiv 2025](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.01.30.25321378.full.pdf)
- **Burnout:** 32.9% (MBI-SS, 2 colleges). — [North East Medical Journal](https://journal.nemc.edu.bd/?p=6878). Also [JAFMC 2025](https://www.banglajol.info/index.php/JAFMC/article/view/84064). PHQ-9 study of 1,921 students in 5 colleges. — [Chattogram Med J](https://banglajol.info/index.php/CMEJ/article/view/84274)
- **Ragging:** at Sher-e-Bangla MC, a female student was abused in a hall room at night by self-described Chhatra League leaders. A High Court order (2020) required anti-ragging committees in all institutions, but most did not comply. — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/local-news/853zp03ph9); [Prothom Alo editorial](https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/opinion/editorial/ox6e5sxbvn) (dates not verified)
- **Government-set private fees:** admission fee raised 20% to about Tk1.95 million (19.5 lakh) from 2022–23, monthly tuition Tk10,000 and internship fee Tk180,000. — [Financial Express, Feb 2023](https://mail.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/bangladesh-raises-admission-fees-by-20pc-for-private-medical-college-students-1677032870?amp=true). That implies about 19.5 + 6.0 + 1.8 ≈ 27 lakh in total (my arithmetic).
- **Reported range:** the Daily Star (Oct 2024) reports BDT 30–45 lakh for the full course, with BDT 20–24 lakh paid upfront at most colleges. — [Daily Star Campus](https://www.thedailystar.net/campus/news/do-we-have-too-many-medical-colleges-3724766). This conflicts with the "21–25 lakh" figure in the brief. 21–25 lakh roughly matches the upfront/admission component, not the total.
- **Foreign students (private colleges, DGME):** peak 2,096, then 1,669 (2023–24), then 1,187 (2024–25), and still falling. — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/kh2l2zl57m). The DGME 2023 circular caps foreign students at 45% of allocated seats. — [DGME notice](https://dgme.portal.gov.bd//sites/default/files/files/dgme.portal.gov.bd/notices/a61e0c07_15a3_4b44_8871_f39eff5aac9b/2023-02-11-06-33-8c86f93d863b7808ede5be2da5b799be.pdf). Most foreign students are from India and Nepal.

### Inferences
- An anonymous ragging and harassment reporting tool mapped to anti-ragging committees, with escalation tracking, has a legal hook (the 2020 HC order) and a clear compliance gap.
- A peer-support or wellbeing check-in for medical students exists widely abroad. It is a weaker hackathon pick unless tied to a specific workflow such as exam periods.

### Gaps
- No 2025–26 government fee notice found. No national survey on hostel safety.

## A6. What access do students and faculty have to journals, and what is the research capacity?

### Takeaway
Bangladesh is listed as Research4Life (HINARI) Group A, which means free access for eligible not-for-profit institutions. Integrity problems are documented: at least 325 retractions from 2019 to June 2026, no national anti-plagiarism policy in medicine, and promotion tied to publication counts.

### Cited Findings
- **Research4Life:** Bangladesh is listed as Group A (free). Group B pays USD 1,500 a year. — [Research4Life eligibility (mirror)](https://tampere2020.iamcr.org/www.research4life.org/access/eligibility/index.html); [FAO AGORA eligibility](https://www.fao.org/agora/eligibility) [date of list uncertain; LDC graduation may affect future status]
- **Retractions:** at least 325 papers by Bangladeshi researchers were retracted from 2019 to June 2026. — [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/education/turning-research-into-a-farce); [Daily Star editorial](https://www.thedailystar.net/opinion/editorial/news/dishonest-academic-practices-must-stop-4238426)
- **Medicine:** no anti-plagiarism policy in Bangladesh medicine, and a set number of publications is required for doctors' promotion. — [J Medicine (DMC), BanglaJOL](https://banglajol.info/index.php/JOM/article/view/8422/6254)
- **BSMMU pilot:** among PG students, only 36% knew that a paraphrased sentence needs a reference; this rose to 72% after training. — search summary of the same literature set [uncertain exact citation]
- **E-libraries:** e-libraries came with the 2023 simulation-lab rollout. — [Dhaka Tribune 2023](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/309069/simulation-labs-e-libraries-at-8-medical-colleges)

### Gaps
- No data on HINARI activation or usage by Bangladeshi colleges. No medical research funding figures (BMRC budget) found.

## A7. What about faculty development, teaching workload and digital teaching (incl. COVID)?

### Takeaway
CME Dhaka (est. 1983, under DGME) is the national faculty-development body (teaching-methodology workshops, MMEd). A study found 95% of 331 teachers had teaching-methodology training, about 80% of it via CME. Yet in another study, 62% had attended no faculty development in the prior year. COVID-era online teaching failed mainly on internet cost and quality and on practical teaching.

### Cited Findings
- **CME role:** est. 1983 at Mohakhali with WHO/UNDP support. Trains teachers of medical, dental, nursing and allied institutes. Runs a 2-year MMEd (since 2004–05, now under BSMMU/BMU). Set up Medical Education Units in 8 colleges. — [Banglapedia](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Centre_for_Medical_Education); [CME portal](https://cme.portal.gov.bd/site/page/b2a781e6-e8d9-472e-8235-2f4d00c4db17)
- **Coverage:** 95% of 331 teachers had formal training in Teaching Methodology & Assessment, about 80% from CME. — [BJME](https://www.banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/87079/56477). However, 38% had attended a faculty-development activity within 1 year and 62% none. — [BJME](https://www.banglajol.info/index.php/BJME/article/view/49245)
- **COVID online teaching:**
  - Mugda MC (2021): students cited poor internet (67%), data cost (65%) and ineffective practical/anatomy teaching (59%); 85% of teachers named practical/bedside teaching as the main barrier. — [SEAJME](https://seajme.sljol.info/articles/10.4038/seajme.v18i2.637)
  - Chattagram Int'l MC: 58% of teachers and 74% of students never felt online classes substitute for face-to-face. — [BanglaJOL CMOSHMCJ](https://banglajol.info/index.php/CMOSHMCJ/article/view/59759) [attribution of figures to this paper via search summary]
- **DGHS COVID-19 course:** a 7-module course for doctors on a2i Muktopaath, with quiz certification. — [teachers.gov.bd mirror](https://teachers.gov.bd/content/details/543477); [South-South Galaxy](https://my.southsouth-galaxy.org/en/solutions/detail/muktopaath-to-train-health-professionals)

### Inferences
- Faculty pain points match the evidence: high vacancy, so overload; signature-only assessment; no structured student feedback system found. A faculty-side MVP could be an auto-generated, blueprint-mapped item bank with item analysis (difficulty and discrimination index) for in-course exams, or an anonymous structured student-feedback tool per teaching session.
- Any tool should work under low bandwidth and on mobile first, given the COVID-era barriers.

### Gaps
- No study found on structured student evaluation of teaching in Bangladesh, or on the accreditation-paperwork burden on faculty.

## A8. What are the quality issues in nursing, midwifery and paramedic (MATS) education?

### Takeaway
Nursing institutions rose from 87 (2010) to 453 (2025), 383 of them private, with weak oversight. Few private nursing colleges have their own hospital (e.g. 2 of 10 in Mirpur), there is a shortage of qualified instructors, and a nurse-teacher training centre opened only in 2025 (30 graduates). MATS: 16 government schools and 200+ private ones, with weak quality oversight; the "diploma doctor" title conflict led to 2025 strikes.

### Cited Findings
- **Nursing scale:** 87 nursing institutions in 2010 and 453 in 2025 (70 public, 383 private). More than 36,000 students admitted a year. Nurses employed are about 18% of the requirement. — [Bonik Barta (citing DGHS Health Workforce Strategy 2024)](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/2AkGPWv89oGNdGYp)
- **Private nursing colleges:** about 50 private nursing colleges in 4 Dhaka areas, most lacking infrastructure and teachers. Only 2 of 10 in Mirpur have their own hospitals. — [Daily Star editorial](https://www.thedailystar.net/opinion/editorial/news/nursing-colleges-are-struggling-amid-poor-oversight-3447461)
- **Instructors and practice:** shortage of qualified instructors and minimal hands-on clinical training. — [Daily Star](https://tds-images.thedailystar.net/health/news/nursing-sector-plagued-challenges-3711951). Midwifery internships lack clinical preceptors. — [Prothom Alo op-ed](https://en.prothomalo.com/opinion/wgruo5821l)
- **Teacher training:** a Nurse Teachers Training Centre in Mohakhali (2025) had 1 batch of 30 complete a 1-year course, with 60 more in training. — search summary of [UNB](https://unb.com.bd/news/tag/157343) [uncertain]
- **MATS:** 16 government MATS and 200+ non-government. Diploma in Medicine Faculty is 3 years plus 1-year internship. Entry needs SSC GPA 2.5. Oversight of quality is weak. — [Daily Star, 20 Mar 2025](https://epfs.thedailystar.net/TDS/2025/03/20/Bangladesh/CAM/5_07/0353045d247e4846_07.pdf). The "diploma doctor" protests of 2025 are covered in [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/features/panorama/bangladeshs-healthcare-mess-how-can-government-clean-it-1086316). A 2016 study found MATS rely mostly on classroom teaching, with essay exams the common form of assessment. — [BRAC U](https://dspace.bracu.ac.bd/items/7d2975b4-67cf-41a5-9f61-16f88e25aecd)

- Pharmacy and BDS-specific education quality data were not researched in depth (time limit).

## B. Which foreign med-ed innovations are proven, and does Bangladesh already have each?

### Takeaway
The clearest gaps in Bangladesh, with strong foreign precedent, are:
1. a digital logbook/e-portfolio (UK Horus; India PGMER-2023 e-logbook);
2. digital OSCE scoring (Qpercom);
3. a Bangla adaptive question bank for FCPS/residency, India's Marrow/PrepLadder model (no verified Bangladeshi equivalent found);
4. transparent PG seat counselling (India MCC);
5. a smartphone serious game for neonatal or emergency skills (LIFE, Kenya, with RCT).

Global apps (Complete Anatomy, AMBOSS, Touch Surgery) are usable in Bangladesh but not localised.

### Cited Findings (foreign evidence)
- **Horus ePortfolio (UK):** used by all foundation schools in England (NHSE); Turas in Scotland, Wales and NI. Records PDPs, supervised learning events and reflections, reviewed at ARCP. Trainees need at least 60 h of learning (at least 30 core). — [KSS Deanery](https://kss.hee.nhs.uk/horus-eportfolio/); [Yorks & Humber](https://yorksandhumberdeanery.nhs.uk/node/200)
- **India e-logbook:** PGMER-2023 reportedly requires e-logbooks for PG trainees. Undergraduate CBME logbooks are still largely paper (e.g. IAPSM logbook). Vendors such as Academe ePortfolios market NMC/NBEMS-compliant apps. — [eportfolios.in guide (vendor)](https://eportfolios.in/resources/guides/logbooks-vs-eportfolios); [IAPSM logbook](https://www.iapsm.org/pdf/IAPSM-Logbook-FINAL.pdf) [regulatory claim from vendor; NMC notice not verified]
- **Qpercom Observe (Ireland):** tablet OSCE scoring with psychometrics and borderline regression. Used by Karolinska, Dundee, NUS and Sheffield (since 2016). Vendor says 30+ institutions. — [Karolinska staff page](https://staff.ki.se/education-support/assessment-evaluation/assessment-of-learning/digital-osce-and-qpercom); [Qpercom](https://www.qpercom.com/about/)
- **Marrow / PrepLadder (India, NEET-PG):** Marrow claims 500k+ students and 18,500 MCQs (self-reported) and reached 200k users in 2018. — [Marrow about](https://marrow.com/about-us); [Medical Dialogues 2018](https://education.medicaldialogues.in/neet-pg-preparation-platform-marrow-crosses-a-user-base-of-200000-students). Unacademy bought PrepLadder for about $50M in July 2020 (85k active subscribers then). — [YourStory 2020](https://yourstory.com/2020/07/edtech-startup-unacademy-prepladder)
- **India MCC NEET-PG counselling:** online counselling for 50% All-India-Quota seats. Institutes verify a public seat matrix; candidates fill ranked choices fresh each round; 4 rounds including a stray-vacancy round, all at mcc.nic.in. — [Careers360 2025](https://news.careers360.com/mcc-neet-pg-2025-counselling-dates-out-round-1-mcc-nic-in-aiq-state-seats-sc-hearing-choice-filling-begins-complete-schedule/amp). The US NRMP match algorithm was not separately verified in this session.
- **LIFE (Kenya/Oxford):** free 3D smartphone serious game for neonatal resuscitation; rolled out to 5,000+ health workers. — [Advances in Simulation 2022](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8744048/); [Oxford](https://www.medsci.ox.ac.uk/news/new-covid-19-simulation-training-for-smartphones-helps-african-healthcare-workers-save-lives). An RCT of adaptive vs standard feedback was published (Tuti et al., JMIR 2020, PMID 32628115); the protocol enrolled 541, of whom 238 reached the endpoint by Jul 2019. — [JMIR Res Protoc 2019](https://www.researchprotocols.org/2019/7/e13034). I could not retrieve the RCT effect sizes (page blocked).
- **Body Interact (virtual patients):** a pilot RCT (VIRTUE-Pilot, Chile, n=50) found slightly higher OSCE scores but no significant difference. — [PMC10663973 (Cureus 2023)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10663973/). The rest of the evidence is mostly vendor-hosted or pre-post.
- **Touch Surgery (Medtronic):** free app, used by over 1M HCPs. Small RCTs exist, e.g. catheterisation with 27 students (2017). — [PMC5725819](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5725819). A Rwanda tendon RCT is listed by the vendor. — [Medtronic literature](https://www.medtronic.com/en-us/healthcare-professionals/specialties/touch-surgery/clinical-literature.html)
- **Anatomage:** deployed in India (VMMC/Safdarjung; Manipal Tata MC, Aug 2024) and the Philippines (only 6 schools nationally). — [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/medical-device/lite/maverick-simulation-installs-3d-anatomage-table-at-safdarjung-hospital-18011); [Silliman U](https://su.edu.ph/?p=4224)
- **Safe Delivery App (Maternity Foundation):** offline WHO-based childbirth guidance, used in about 40 LMICs. UNFPA reportedly funded a Bangladesh adaptation (being prepared as of 2017). — [UNFPA](https://asiapacific.unfpa.org/en/node/57817); [Globalnyt (Danish)](https://globalnyt.dk/safe-delivery-app-ruster-sundhedspersonale-til-redde-liv)
- **Helping Babies Breathe:** Bangladesh adopted it nationally in 2010; by 2013 there were 1,772 ToT sessions and 21,000+ birth attendants trained in 55+ districts. The digital "HBB Prompt" app improved 6-month skill retention in Uganda. — [MCHIP Bangladesh HBB report](https://www.mchip.net/sites/default/files/HBB%20PD%20Report_Bangladesh.pdf); [MUST Uganda](https://ir.must.ac.ug/items/2b3424a4-4419-4c0c-9ed6-8f09ef566302)
- **Project ECHO:** Bangladesh has palliative-care ECHO programmes (Rohingya response: 250 clinicians; 5 courses, 60 sessions, 639 enrolments). — [J Med Educ Curric Dev 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9403457); [MSF science portal](https://scienceportal.msf.org/api/assets/7883/download/14121)

### Inferences (shortlist-ready, ≤3 people, 24–48h MVP)
1. **Digital internship logbook plus supervisor e-sign-off (group C/D).** The pain is quantified: more than 66% signature-only assessment and about 56% negative on logbook use. Foreign precedent: Horus and the India PGMER e-logbook. Bangladesh status: absent.
2. **Tablet OSPE/OSCE scorer with examiner-variance analytics (group D).** Directly addresses the documented between-college score variance. Precedent: Qpercom.
3. **FCPS Part I adaptive question bank with blueprint mapping and spaced repetition (group C).** About 11,000 candidates a year; MCQ/SBA-only exam; a fragmented local market.
4. **PG seat-matrix and allocation transparency portal (group C).** India's MCC model, and allowance and placement grievances drive strikes. Depends on BCPS data, so a demo would use mock data.
5. **Bangla smartphone serious game for neonatal resuscitation / PPH for nursing, midwifery and MATS students (group C).** RCT-backed LIFE precedent, plus Bangladesh's existing HBB national programme that has no digital refresher.
6. **Accreditation evidence tracker for BMEAC's 11 criteria (group D).** About 100 colleges need self-assessment reports.

- Bangladeshi Facebook/coaching FCPS platforms ("Genesis", "Mediprep") and med-student apps ("Medivoice", "Shornolota") were not verifiable from public web sources.
